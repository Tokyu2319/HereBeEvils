> [!note] *The information presented in this article is information provided from █████-████.*  
  
**Phana** is a subatomic particle that exhibits photon-like behavior. Phana carries energy and is metastable once generated. According to █████-████‘s information, durations of phana metastability ranges from approximately 60 milliseconds to 4 years. Upon destabilization, phana transfers its energy into the surrounding environment. Phana possesses wavelength and frequency similar to photons, determined at generation and through interaction with matter. The thaumical practice for converting mana into phana and releasing it is known as **phanagenesis**.
