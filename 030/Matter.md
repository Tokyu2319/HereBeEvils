## Hylogenesis  
> Main article: [Hylogenesis](../133.43/Hylogenesis.md)  
  
**Hylogenesis** is the thaumical process of converting [mana](./Mana.md) into matter. According to prevailing theories, the Big Bang itself was the result of a [being](./Being.md) hylogenizing all matter in the universe. Such a being is believed to emerge from a sufficient degree of [complexity](../501/Theory%20of%20complexity.md), suggesting that the universe’s creation may have been an act of hylogenesis on a cosmic scale.
