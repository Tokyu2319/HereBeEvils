> [!note]  
> *Values presented in this article are theoretical approximations and serve to express the relationship between anima development and thaumic output.*  
  
**Anima** is understood to be the result of [enrichment](./Experience.md) and the source of [mana](./Mana.md). It is the last constituent to emerge, forming once the [cogito](./Cogito.md) is able to interpret experiences. Animae do not obey the laws governing matter and energy, only interacting with the universe through thaumaturgy.  
  
## Mana release  
Mana Release is the process of releasing mana from the anima, through the [corpus](./Corpus.md), and into the universe. Mana release is influenced by two primary factors:  
- the mana capacity and the generative ability of the anima,  
- and the agency of cogito that governs the precision of conversion.  
  
### Mana conversion costs  
Mana is released through the corpus and may be converted into specialized states during externalization. The known base costs are as follows:  
- Phana: base cost of 1 mana per ’unit’  
- Manon: base cost of 5 mana per ’unit’  
- Armana: base cost of 7 mana per ’unit’  
- Matter: base cost of ~67 mana per ’unit of matter’  
  
### Mana capacity and generation  
The amount of mana a being may contain and generate is determined primarily by [the value of their anima](./Experience.md#Comparative%20values).  
  
- Mana capacity (MC) = 10 × AV  
- Mana generation (MG) = √AV × S per cycle  
  
Where:  
- MC = mana capacity  
- $MG$ = mana generation  
- AV = anima value  
- S = sanity, a coefficient representing the current psychological, emotional, and cognitive condition of a being, with a baseline value of 1 and a practical range of 1 to 45.  
- Cycle = a unit of regenerative time approximately comparable to one hour on Earth  
  
For example, a being with 2,500 AV would possess:  
- a mana capacity of 25,000 mana  
- a generation rate of 50 mana per cycle, assuming ’S’ is equal to 1  
  
## Mana exhaustion  
Mana exhaustion occurs when a being depletes its internal mana reserves beyond sustainable limits. Because mana contributes to the cohesion between the cogito, corpus, and anima, severe depletion may destabilize the integrity of the three constituents themselves. Early symptoms commonly include:  
- physical weakness,  
- cognitive impairment,  
- emotional destabilization,  
- sensory distortion,  
- and diminished awareness of self.  
  
Extreme exhaustion will result in decoherence of the three constituents without necessary harm to either the corpus or the cogito. Decoherence will generally result in death.  
  
### Mana exhaustion blindness  
Paradoxically, beings whose anima are of higher value, are often more vulnerable to catastrophic exhaustion despite their immense mana reserves. The vast scale of their reserves may obscure awareness of depletion until decoherence has already begun.  
  
This blindness cannot be felt by higher beings; it can only be known indirectly, by those who have studied how their own depletion behaves. Those who possess this understanding tend to release their mana deliberately, stopping short of the threshold they cannot sense. Those who do not remain wholly unaware of how close they stand to it—and may discover the limit only by crossing it, pouring their entire reserve into a single act and collapsing dead the instant it is spent, with no warning ever having reached them.  
  
## Transience  
Transience is a state where an anima is without its cogito and corpus. Transience is a natural state associated with death, considered a form of afterlife. Animae that exist in this state are referred to as transient anima. In this state, transient animae are unable to gain experiences* and, because animae do not obey the laws governing matter and energy, their position will remain fixed in space until a thaumical disturbance alters it.
