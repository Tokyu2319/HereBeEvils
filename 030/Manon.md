> [!note] *The information presented in this article is information provided from █████-████.*  
  
**Manon** is a subatomic particle capable of exerting an attractive force. Once generated, manon is metastable, with natural metastability durations ranging from approximately 0.8 to 5 seconds. Upon destabilization, a single manon particle generates a transient attractive force of approximately 12 millinewtons on a 1 kg reference mass, drawing matter within an approximate radius of 12 centimeters toward the particle’s point of origin. The attractive interaction persists for 122 milliseconds, after which the manon particle ceases to exist and the interaction terminates. The resulting acceleration is inversely proportional to the mass of the attracted object.  
  
The thaumical practice for converting mana into manon and releasing it is known as **manogenesis**.  
  
## Manokinesis  
> Main article: [Manokinesis](../133.43/Manokinesis.md)  
  
Manokinesis is the thaumical practice of stabilizing and manipulating manon under a [cogito](./Cogito.md) or an armature. It allows practitioners to suspend the natural metastability of manon and control its attractive force.  
  
## Manon fields  
A **manon field** is formed when two or more stable manon particles exist within 12 centimeters of one another or satisfy the applicable interaction-region overlap condition. Particles separated by no more than 12 centimeters are considered directly connected, with indirectly connected particles constituting part of the same field.  
  
A field can extend considerably beyond 12 centimeters through a connected arrangement of particles. Its overall dimensions are therefore not limited by the interaction radius of an individual particle.  
  
Manon particles can pass through one another without interacting. Fields may consequently form complex configurations, including branches, loops, intersections, sheets, and ropes.  
  
The destabilization of a single particle within a connected field induces the destabilization of all other particles belonging to that field, triggering their respective transient attractive interactions. Individual particles must first be separated from their connected field to undergo deliberate destabilization without affecting the remaining particles.  
  
Practitioners can maintain multiple disconnected fields simultaneously, although maintaining a single connected field is generally easier.  
  
### Field interference  
Manon fields belonging to different practitioners can destabilize one another through physical connection. Interference occurs when particles belonging to separate fields come within 12 centimeters of one another.  
  
When an invading practitioner successfully destabilizes a defending practitioner’s field, the invading practitioner retains their own field. If the defending practitioner successfully prevents the interference, both fields destabilize.  
  
Consequently, field interference has two possible outcomes: the destabilization of the defending practitioner’s field alone, or the destabilization of both fields. A defending practitioner cannot destabilize an invading field while retaining their own.  
  
The vulnerability of a field depends partly on its configuration and exposure. A large quantity of manon maintained close to the practitioner’s body is comparatively difficult to target. Extending the field into configurations such as sheets or ropes makes it more exposed to interference.  
  
## Interaction with matter  
Manon particles can be obstructed by sufficiently dense matter. Their ability to pass through a material depends on both its density and composition.  
  
Lead is considered capable of obstructing manon particles at sufficient thickness. A thickness of approximately one light-year of lead is tentatively estimated to be sufficient to prevent their passage.  
  
Manon particles are invisible to the naked eye. Their positions can be inferred from their effects, while specialized equipment may allow direct observation or detection.  
  
The precise material properties governing manon obstruction remain incompletely characterized.
