A **being** is an entity possessing its own [corpus](./Corpus.md), [cogito](./Cogito.md), and [anima](./Anima.md), which are collectively called its constituents.  
  
# Constituents  
Corpus is the body through which a being interacts with the universe. Cogito is the awareness that interprets that interaction. Anima is what the [experiences](./Experience.md) gathered by cogito accumulate into, and is known chiefly through its effects on [mana](../133.43/Mana.md) and thaumaturgy. Each has its own article.  
  
# Order and cohesion  
The constituents are held to emerge in sequence, corpus first, then cogito, then anima, as set out in the [theory of complexity](../501/Theory%20of%20complexity.md). Mana contributes the cohesion between them, referred to as mana reserves, and severe depletion will cause decoherence (see [mana exhaustion](./Anima.md#Mana%20exhaustion)).
