 **Mana** is the required resource to maintain the coherence between [anima](./Anima.md), [cogito](./Cogito.md), and [corpus](./Corpus.md). Mana is a subatomic particle that is stable inside a [being’s](./Being.md) and metastable outside of it. Mana is inherently transformative and may convert into specialized states. The principal forms are [phana](./Phana.md), [manon](./Manon.md), [armana](./Armana.md), and [matter](./Matter.md). Each has its own article.  
  
## Raw mana  
Raw mana are mana particles existing independently from its anima and outside its specialized states. Raw mana will naturally convert itself into phana or manon when it enters their fields. Raw mana is inherently temporary and subject to mana decay.  
  
### Raw mana decay  
Raw mana cannot return to the anima from which it was released and undergoes irreversible decay at an apparently constant rate. This decay eventually destabilizes the raw mana, causing it to release its remaining energy through one of several known manifestations of destabilization:  
- radiant flashes and thermal discharge,  
- transient force reactions,  
- or spontaneous armagenesis or hylogenesis.  
  
These manifestations correspond behaviorally to phana, manon, armana and matter respectively.  
  
Additionally, armana may prolong the stability of mana and delay decay through armatures*.
