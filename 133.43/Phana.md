**Phana** (/ˈfɑː.nə/) is a subatomic particle that exhibits photon-like behavior. Phana carries energy and is metastable once generated. According to Ramanujan‘s information, durations of phana metastability ranges from approximately 60 milliseconds to 4 years. Upon destabilization, phana transfers its energy into the surrounding environment. Phana possesses wavelength and frequency similar to photons, determined at generation and through interaction with matter. The thaumical process for converting mana into phana and releasing it is known as **phanagenesis**.  
  
## Phana and anima  
Phana is able to reflect off an unobstructed [anima](../120/Anima.md). Because an anima is usually enclosed within a [corpus](../120/Corpus.md), and a corpus blocks both phana and light, the anima of a living being is generally difficult to illuminate. Transient animae are not enclosed in a corpus and are generally easy to illuminate (see [transience](../120/Anima.md#Transience)).  
  
Practitioners use this property to estimate the value of an anima. Phana is shone onto the anima, and the amount of phana it reflects is observed. A brighter reflection indicates an anima of higher value. An anima of low density allows more phana to avoid it, and so reflects less. The relationship between value and brightness is compressive, so the reflection resolves only broad ranges and not exact values. This is consistent with the approximate [comparative values](../120/Experience.md#Comparative%20values) used by thaumaturgists.  
  
An illuminated anima appears spherical, with an apparent radius of approximately 12 centimeters. The Library does not know whether this appearance corresponds to the actual size of the anima.  
  
Shining phana along the path a planet has traveled can reveal the transient animae of beings who died on it. Each appears as a reflection, and their number and brightness may be used to estimate the planet's death toll.
