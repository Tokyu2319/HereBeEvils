An **anecho** (/aˈne.kho/) is a [being](../120/Being.md) born with a birthmark and an ability that Library Alhazen cannot explain within its current understanding of physics and thaumics. Some anechoi also lack [counterparts](../523/Sympan.md#Counterparts), though this is not required.

## Birthmarks  
Every anecho is born with a single **anecho birthmark**. The presence of a birthmark is regarded as proof that its bearer is an anecho (see [Charlotte](../920/Charlotte%20(anecho).md)).  
  
Birthmarks are unique to each bearer in position, appearance, and behavior. Most respond in some way to the use of the bearer's ability, commonly by illuminating. The form of the response varies between individuals, and a birthmark may respond to the state of an ability rather than to its use.  
  
A birthmark belongs to its bearer and not to the [corpus](../120/Corpus.md) on which it appears. When a bearer comes to occupy a different corpus, the birthmark disappears from the former corpus and appears on the new one. This has been observed both through understood processes, such as [corpus assimilation](../120/Corpus.md#Corpus%20assimilation), and through their abilities. The cause of the change has not been found to affect the transfer.  
  
When an anecho dies, their birthmark disappears. Because a birthmark does not persist in [transience](../120/Anima.md#Transience), it is not considered bound to the [anima](../120/Anima.md) alone. Which constituent it is bound to has not been determined.  
  
## Abilities  
Each anecho is born with at least one ability. The Library describes these abilities as unexplained, and the classification does not establish that they operate outside the mana system. Some documented abilities draw upon mana, while others depend on resources unrelated to it.  
  
Documented abilities differ considerably:  
- They may be active, passive, or composed of both.  
- They are not necessarily under the control of their bearer.  
- Their scope ranges from direct physical contact to an entire [sympan](../523/Sympan.md).  
- They may belong to anechoi that are not humanoid, including anechoi possessing an [armature](./Armature.md).  
  
Some abilities depend upon the structure of the universe described in the article on sympanta. No unifying theme among abilities has been established.  
  
## Counterparts  
Some anechoi have no counterparts. Others have counterparts and are nevertheless anecho, and at least one documented ability depends upon the existence of counterparts. The absence of counterparts is therefore regarded as a trait of some anechoi and not as a requirement of the classification.

## Distribution  
Four documented anechoi, Olive, Charlotte, Níu, and Nelu, reside in sympan (0, 0, -2), which holds the most anechoi the Library has documented. No cause for this concentration is known. The figure reflects the Library's observations and does not establish that anechoi are more common in that sympan.
