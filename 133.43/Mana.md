 **Mana** is the required resource to maintain the coherence between [anima](../030/Anima.md), [cogito](../030/Cogito.md), and [corpus](../030/Corpus.md). Mana is a subatomic particle that is stable inside a [being’s](../030/Being.md) and metastable outside of it. Mana is inherently transformative and may convert into specialized states. The principal forms are [phana](./Phana.md), [manon](./Manon.md), [armana](./Armana.md), and [matter](../030/Matter.md). Each has its own article.  
  
## Raw mana  
Raw mana are mana particles existing independently from their anima and outside their specialized states. Raw mana will naturally convert itself into phana or manon when it enters their fields. Raw mana is inherently temporary and subject to mana decay.  
  
### Raw mana decay  
Raw mana cannot return to the anima from which it was released and undergoes irreversible decay at an apparently constant rate. This decay eventually destabilizes the raw mana, causing it to release its remaining energy through one of several known manifestations of destabilization:  
- radiant flashes and thermal discharge,  
- transient force reactions,  
- or spontaneous armagenesis or hylogenesis.  
  
These manifestations correspond behaviorally to phana, manon, armana and matter respectively.  
  
Additionally, armana may prolong the stability of mana and delay decay through armatures*.  
  
## Mana release  
Mana Release is the process of releasing mana from the anima, through the corpus, and into the universe. Mana release is influenced by two primary factors:  
- the mana capacity and the generative ability of the anima,  
- and the agency of cogito that governs the precision of conversion.  
  
### Mana conversion costs  
Mana is released through the corpus and may be converted into specialized states during externalization. The known base costs are as follows:  
- Phana: base cost of 1 mana per ’unit’  
- Manon: base cost of 5 mana per ’unit’  
- Armana: base cost of 7 mana per ’unit’  
- Matter: base cost of ~67 mana per ’unit of matter’  
  
### Mana capacity and generation  
The amount of mana a being may contain and generate is determined primarily by [the value of their anima](../030/Experience.md#Comparative%20values).  
  
- Mana capacity ($MC$) = 10 × $AV$  
- Mana generation ($MG$) = √AV × $S$ per cycle  
  
Where:  
- $MC$ = mana capacity  
- $MG$ = mana generation  
- $AV$ = anima value  
- $S$ = sanity, a coefficient representing the current psychological, emotional, and cognitive condition of a being, with a baseline value of 1 and a practical range of 1 to 45.  
- Cycle = a unit of regenerative time approximately comparable to one hour on Earth  
  
For example, a being with 2,500 $AV$ would possess:  
- a mana capacity of 25,000 mana  
- a generation rate of 50 mana per cycle, assuming $S$ is equal to 1
