**Hylogenesis** is the thaumical process of converting [mana](./Mana.md) into [matter](../120/Matter.md). The matter produced is ordinary matter, and its chemistry is not limited. The practice of hylogenesis using the knowledge of living systems is known as [biogenesis](Hylogenesis.md#Biogenesis).

## Surface of creation  
When mana is converted into matter, the matter is created on the surface of the practitioner's [corpus](../120/Corpus.md). This surface may be inside or outside the corpus. Fluids within the corpus, such as stomach acid, are considered part of it. Creating soluble nutrients inside the stomach is therefore an example of hylogenesis performed inside the corpus.  
  
Hylogenesis may be performed by a [being](../120/Being.md) or by an [armature](./Armature.md). For armatures, the matter is created on a desired surface of the armature.  
  
## States of matter  
Gaseous and liquid matter do not require [corpus extension](../120/Corpus.md#Corpus%20extension). Solid matter requires it to remain stable, since newly created solids otherwise lack structural continuity. Gold created on the skin without corpus extension, for example, is flaky and brittle.  
  
## Techniques for solids  
Two techniques are used for solid hylogenesis:  
- **Lamination** is hylogenesis in layers, creating matter on top of matter inside a fluid corpus. It is regarded as an automatic process of corpus extension.  
- **Extrusion** is hylogenesis performed through the deliberate use of corpus extension.  
  
## Thermal byproduct  
Hylogenesis releases thermal energy as a byproduct. A liter of water created without intervention has a temperature of 17°C. The baseline temperature varies by substance, and water is the documented reference.  
  
Skilled practitioners can adjust the byproduct at the moment of hylogenesis, producing matter colder than 0°C. The energy removed is released into the surroundings. Matter can be heated after its creation by agitating it through [manokinesis](./Manokinesis.md).  
  
Matter of extreme temperature harms the practitioner. Practitioners whose corpus is made of a temperature-resistant material are not at risk of being harmed.  
  
## Spontaneous hylogenesis  
Raw mana that destabilizes into matter (see [raw mana decay](./Mana.md#Raw%20mana%20decay)) follows a separate rule from the one described in this article.  
  
## Biogenesis  
**Biogenesis** is hylogenesis that requires knowledge of multiple biological disciplines as well as anatomy. It can be performed on oneself, or on others through corpus extension. Its forms are commonly divided into three tiers of increasing difficulty.  
  
### Basic biogenesis  
The most basic form of biogenesis is **nutrient satiation**, the creation of nutrients within a corpus. Its most practical uses are providing nutrients to garden plants and, in an emergency, trading one's own mana to provide nutrients for one's own corpus.  
  
### Reconstructive biogenesis  
Reconstructive biogenesis includes repairing tissue, creating nerve fibers, and attaching lost limbs. When performed on another being, it relies on corpus extension to integrate tissue into the practitioner's corpus (see [corpus extension](../120/Corpus.md#Corpus%20extension)).  
  
### Organ biogenesis  
Organ biogenesis is the full creation of organs such as the eyes and the brain. Most practitioners die before they are able to perform it on themselves.  
  
A brain created by biogenesis is a blank [cerebrum](../120/Corpus.md#Corpus-corpus%20and%20corpus-cerebrum). The most documented case of organ biogenesis comes from observations of [Charlotte](../920/Charlotte%20(anecho).md), who has maintained her [cogito](../120/Cogito.md) through severe damage to her cerebrum, including a bullet to the head, for as long as she persevered. In these cases her cogito supplied memories to the blank portions of her rebuilt cerebrum (see [the memories of a corpus](../120/Corpus.md#The%20memories%20of%20a%20corpus)). The mechanism by which her cogito and her cogito’s memories persists has not been determined.
