## Self-consistency mechanism  
According to observer accounts of events involving [Charlotte’s](../920/Charlotte%20(anecho).md) manipulation of time, the universe seems to exhibit a self-consistency mechanism that prevents temporal paradoxes from occurring. In her account of the events, Charlotte explained her attempts to produce a paradox were circumvented by changes in the course of events, including influences on the intentions and decisions of individuals involved. Charlotte explained that these influences occurred subconsciously, leading the individuals to make decisions that prevented the paradox without apparent awareness of the mechanism, though Charlotte herself was aware. Charlotte stated that she, too, was affected, but was aware of it.  
  
The observations suggest that temporal paradox prevention does not necessarily prohibit temporal manipulation, but instead constrains its outcomes to preserve consistency across time. The underlying mechanism and the extent of its influence on causality and individual agency remain undetermined.
