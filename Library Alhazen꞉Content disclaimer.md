<span style="font-size: 32px;">LIBRARY ALHAZEN PRIORITIZES THE SAFETY OF ITS READERS</span>  
  
Library Alhazen is an extensive collection of articles, documents, records, and other written materials covering a wide variety of subjects. Its collection may include information that some readers find disturbing, offensive, distressing, inappropriate, or otherwise objectionable.  
  
Library Alhazen does not guarantee that all of its content will be suitable for every reader. Its materials are made available for informational, educational, historical, and other purposes, and readers are encouraged to exercise their own discretion when accessing them.  
  
Some materials may contain:  
  
- Descriptions, illustrations, photographs, or other depictions of violence, injury, death, or human suffering.  
- Profane, vulgar, discriminatory, or otherwise offensive language.  
- Discussions of sexuality, anatomy, bodily functions, and other mature subjects.  
- Accounts of traumatic experiences, abuse, disasters, warfare, and other distressing events.  
- Information concerning dangerous activities, substances, or circumstances.  
- Discussions of controversial, sensitive, or emotionally difficult subjects.  
- Information that may be unsettling to readers with particular experiences, sensitivities, or personal circumstances.  
  
The presence of such material does not necessarily indicate that Library Alhazen endorses, encourages, or approves of the subject being discussed.  
  
Reader safety and discretion  
  
Library Alhazen recognizes that its materials may affect readers differently. Something considered ordinary or informative by one reader may be distressing or harmful to another.  
  
Readers are therefore encouraged to consider their personal circumstances and well-being before accessing material they suspect may be unsuitable for them.  
  
Readers are not obligated to continue reading any material that causes them discomfort, distress, or a sense of danger. They may discontinue their reading at any time.  
  
Library Alhazen considers the well-being of its readers more important than the completeness of any individual reading experience.  
  
Accuracy and reliability of information  
  
Library Alhazen is an ongoing collection of information. Its articles may contain inaccuracies, outdated statements, incomplete explanations, conflicting accounts, or information that has not yet been adequately examined.  
  
Although efforts may be made to improve the quality and reliability of its materials, Library Alhazen does not guarantee that every statement, reference, illustration, or conclusion is entirely accurate.  
  
Readers should exercise critical judgment and, where appropriate, consult additional sources before relying on information found in the Library.  
  
The inclusion of a subject in the Library does not establish its importance, credibility, authenticity, or factual accuracy.  
  
Sensitive and potentially dangerous information  
  
Certain subjects documented by Library Alhazen may concern activities, materials, circumstances, or procedures capable of causing harm.  
  
Such information may be presented for historical, educational, scientific, descriptive, or other legitimate purposes. Its presence should not be interpreted as an encouragement to reproduce, attempt, or participate in the activities described.  
  
Readers should not treat potentially dangerous information as instructions for real-world action without appropriate knowledge, training, and precautions.  
  
Library Alhazen reserves the ability to restrict, modify, remove, or otherwise handle materials when their presentation creates an unreasonable risk to reader safety.  
  
Medical, legal, and professional information  
  
Articles concerning medicine, mental health, law, finance, engineering, and other specialized disciplines are provided for informational purposes.  
  
Such materials are not substitutes for professional assessment, diagnosis, treatment, consultation, or assistance.  
  
Readers facing medical, psychological, legal, financial, or other serious personal circumstances should seek assistance from appropriately qualified individuals or organizations.  
  
Library Alhazen cannot guarantee that its materials adequately address the circumstances of any particular reader.  
  
The responsibility of Library Alhazen  
  
Library Alhazen does not recognize legal compliance as the foundation of its content philosophy. Its governing consideration is the safety and well-being of its readers.  
  
This does not mean that every subject must be concealed, that uncomfortable information must necessarily be removed, or that readers should be prevented from learning about difficult subjects.  
  
Rather, Library Alhazen distinguishes between the preservation and presentation of information and the potential harm that may arise from making that information accessible.  
  
The Library seeks to balance the availability of knowledge with the responsibility of handling it carefully.  
  
Where a conflict arises between the unrestricted presentation of material and the reasonable safety of its readers, Library Alhazen gives priority to reader safety.  
  
The Library may consequently introduce restrictions, warnings, modifications, or removals when it considers them necessary to protect its readers.  
  
No guarantee of a completely safe experience  
  
Despite its precautions, Library Alhazen cannot guarantee that every potentially disturbing, misleading, inappropriate, or harmful element will be identified before a reader encounters it.  
  
Some material may be presented without an individual content warning. The absence of a warning should not be interpreted as confirmation that an article contains nothing potentially objectionable.  
  
Readers should remain mindful of their own well-being when accessing the Library.  
  
Library Alhazen welcomes the identification of material that may pose a risk to its readers and encourages readers to bring such concerns to its attention.  
  
Knowledge should be made accessible, but the safety of those who seek it should never be treated as an afterthought.
