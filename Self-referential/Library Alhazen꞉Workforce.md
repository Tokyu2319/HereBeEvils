**Library Alhazen’s workforce** is composed of partitions of its founder, Sabitha. These individuals constitute the institution’s personnel and participate in its various activities, including research, documentation, archival preservation, expeditions, security operations, defensive measures, agriculture, food production, preparation, preservation, and distribution.  
  
Although staff members share a common biological origin and initial memories dating back to before Library Alhazen’s founding, they are not considered identical individuals in terms of personality, [experiences](../120/Experience.md), or identity.  
  
Each staff member possesses an individual personality, behavioral characteristics, preferences, and capabilities. These differences influence their interactions, activities, and suitability for particular institutional responsibilities.  
  
Staff members are identified through individual titles that describe distinguishing aspects of their personalities or behavioral characteristics. Such titles serve as descriptive designations rather than necessarily representing formal occupational positions.

## Individuality and identity  
Despite their common origin, Library Alhazen’s staff members are recognized as distinct individuals. Their shared biological origin and initial memories do not establish that they possess identical personalities, experiences, intentions, or personal identities.  
  
Following their partitioning, each individual develops through their own experiences and interactions. Differences in accumulated knowledge, environmental circumstances, personal preferences, and behavioral tendencies contribute to the development of distinct identities.  
  
Consequently, the designation of a staff member as a partition of Sabitha does not necessarily imply that the individual should be regarded as interchangeable with the founder or other staff members.  
  
## Titles and designations  
> Main article: [Library Alhazen꞉Title](./Library%20Alhazen%EA%9E%89Title.md)  
  
Each staff member identifies themselves through an individual title describing distinguishing aspects of their personality or behavioral characteristics. These titles serve as their primary means of individual identification within the Library.  
  
Titles may consist of one or more descriptive characteristics, including combinations of seemingly contradictory traits.  
  
## Roles and responsibilities  
Library Alhazen’s staff members participate in a variety of institutional activities and are assigned responsibilities according to the Library’s operational requirements and the capabilities of individual personnel.  
  
Their responsibilities encompass research, documentation, archival preservation, expeditions, security operations, defensive measures, administration, maintenance, and other activities necessary for the institution’s operation.  
  
Individual staff members may specialize in particular fields or participate in multiple areas of activity. The nature and extent of their responsibilities may vary according to their capabilities, experience, interests, and institutional requirements.  
  
A staff member’s personality or individual title does not necessarily determine their occupational responsibilities. Individuals with substantially different behavioral characteristics may perform similar functions, while individuals with similar personalities may participate in entirely different activities.  
  
Some responsibilities may require cooperation between multiple staff members, particularly when activities involve specialized knowledge, extensive resources, or coordinated operations. Other responsibilities may be performed independently.  
  
The distribution of responsibilities is not necessarily permanent. Staff members may undertake different assignments or assume additional responsibilities as institutional requirements change.  
  
## Staff interactions and relations  
Library Alhazen’s staff members interact with one another through their institutional activities, personal relationships, and other forms of social interaction. Although they share a common origin, their individual personalities, experiences, and behavioral characteristics contribute to differences in how they communicate and interact.  
  
Relationships between staff members may vary considerably. Some individuals maintain cooperative and mutually supportive relationships, while others may experience disagreements, rivalries, interpersonal conflicts, or persistent hostility.  
  
Differences in personality and individual preferences may influence the formation and maintenance of these relationships. Staff members are not necessarily compatible with one another, and their shared origin does not establish an expectation of mutual agreement or cooperation.  
  
Interactions may also be influenced by differences in responsibilities, capabilities, attitudes, and approaches to institutional activities. Some staff members may work effectively together despite substantial differences in personality, while others may experience difficulties even when their responsibilities and objectives are similar.  
  
The Library does not consider staff members’ common biological origin sufficient justification for treating their relationships as inherently harmonious. Their interpersonal relationships are regarded as interactions between distinct individuals rather than interactions between interchangeable partitions of a single personality.  
  
## Staff population  
The number of staff members may change over time as a result of the Library’s internal processes and other circumstances affecting its personnel. As of Alima 892, the Library has a recorded staff population of 2,245 individuals.  
  
The staff population encompasses individuals participating in the various activities and operations of the institution. Their responsibilities, personalities, and individual characteristics are not necessarily distributed uniformly across the workforce.  
  
Each staff member is recognized as an individual regardless of their particular responsibilities or degree of participation in the Library’s activities.
