**Library Alhazen’s workforce** is composed of partitions of its founder, Sabitha. These individuals constitute the institution’s personnel and participate in its various activities, including research, documentation, archival preservation, field operations, security operations, defensive measures, administration, maintenance, agriculture, food production, preparation, preservation, and distribution.  
  
Although staff members share a common biological origin and initial memories dating back to before Library Alhazen’s founding, they are recognized as distinct individuals. Each possesses an individual personality, behavioral characteristics, preferences, experiences, and capabilities, which influence their activities, interactions, and suitability for particular responsibilities.  
  
Staff members identify themselves through individual titles describing distinguishing aspects of their personalities or behavioral characteristics. These titles serve as descriptive designations rather than necessarily representing formal occupational positions.

## Individuality and identity  
Despite their common origin, Library Alhazen’s staff members are recognized as distinct individuals. Their identities develop independently following partitioning, shaped by their accumulated [experiences](../120/Experience.md), environmental circumstances, personal preferences, and interactions.  
  
Although individuals may retain memories originating from before the Library’s founding, their subsequent experiences contribute to differences in knowledge, perspectives, intentions, and behavioral tendencies.  
  
Consequently, an individual’s origin as a partition of Sabitha does not establish personal equivalence with the founder or other staff members. Each individual is regarded as possessing their own identity and is treated accordingly.  
  
## Titles and designations

Each staff member identifies themselves through an individual title describing distinguishing aspects of their personality or behavioral characteristics. These titles serve as their primary means of individual identification within the Library.  
  
Titles may consist of one or more descriptive characteristics, including combinations of seemingly contradictory traits.  
  
## Roles and responsibilities  
Library Alhazen’s staff members participate in a variety of institutional activities and are assigned responsibilities according to operational requirements and individual capabilities.  
  
Their responsibilities encompass research, documentation, archival preservation, field operations, security operations, defensive measures, administration, maintenance, agriculture, food production, and other activities necessary for the institution’s operation.  
  
Individual staff members may specialize in particular fields or participate in multiple areas of activity. The nature and extent of their responsibilities may vary according to their capabilities, experience, interests, and institutional requirements.  
  
A staff member’s personality or individual title does not necessarily determine their occupational responsibilities. Individuals with substantially different behavioral characteristics may perform similar functions, while those with similar personalities may participate in entirely different activities.  
  
Some responsibilities require cooperation between multiple staff members, particularly when activities involve specialized knowledge, extensive resources, or coordinated operations. Others may be performed independently.  
  
The distribution of responsibilities is not necessarily permanent. Staff members may undertake different assignments or assume additional responsibilities as institutional requirements change.  
  
## Staff interactions and relations  
Library Alhazen’s staff members interact through their institutional activities, personal relationships, and other forms of social interaction. Their differing personalities, experiences, and preferences contribute to variations in how they communicate and relate to one another.  
  
Relationships between staff members may vary considerably. Some individuals maintain cooperative and mutually supportive relationships, while others may experience disagreements, rivalries, interpersonal conflicts, or persistent hostility.  
  
Interactions may also be influenced by differences in responsibilities, capabilities, attitudes, and approaches to institutional activities. Some staff members may work effectively together despite substantial differences in personality, while others may experience difficulties even when their responsibilities and objectives are similar.  
  
The Library does not consider common biological origin sufficient justification for treating relationships between staff members as inherently harmonious. Their interpersonal relationships are regarded as interactions between distinct individuals rather than interactions between interchangeable partitions of a single personality.  
  
## Staff population  
The number of staff members physically residing within the Library may change over time as a result of its internal processes and other circumstances affecting its personnel. The Library also maintains an extensive field operative workforce, whose members are deployed across various locations and may remain away for indeterminate periods.  
  
Their whereabouts and eventual returns are not always known, making it difficult to maintain an accurate account of their current status. The number of field operatives is estimated to be approximately 1,200,000 individuals, substantially exceeding the population residing within the Library itself. As of Alima 892, the Library has a recorded internal staff population of 2,245 individuals.  
  
The internal staff population encompasses individuals participating in the various activities and operations of the institution. Their responsibilities, personalities, and individual characteristics are not necessarily distributed uniformly across the workforce.
