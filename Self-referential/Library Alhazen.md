**Library Alhazen** is an independent research institution and archival library orbiting [Al-Haytham](../523/Al-Haytham.md). It was founded by Sabitha after she had acquired knowledge concerning almost everything in the universe at that time, initially to catalogue the information she had accumulated.  
  
Having fulfilled its original purpose, Library Alhazen now seeks to document everything in the universe. Its activities encompass the acquisition, examination, documentation, and preservation of information, regardless of its subject or practical significance.  
  
## Structure  
Library Alhazen occupies a single artificial megastructure orbiting Al-Haytham. Its core was created through [hylogenesis](../133.43/Hylogenesis.md), with additional material obtained from nearby planetary bodies. The core provides gravity and an atmosphere.  
  
The megastructure periodically adjusts its orbit through [manokinetic](../133.43/Manokinesis.md) [armature programs](../133.43/Armature.md#As%20a%20program) to maintain alignment with the [institution’s calendar](./Library%20Alhazen%EA%9E%89Alima.md).  
  
The institution maintains connections between different [sympanta](../523/Sympan.md) through [wormholes](../523/Wormhole.md), allowing it to conduct activities across different domains of the universe.  
  
## Governance  
Library Alhazen is governed by its founder, Sabitha, and Library Alhazen Administration. Both are responsible for establishing and maintaining laws and ensuring the safety of the institution’s inhabitants.  
  
Library Alhazen Administration consists of partitions of Sabitha and oversees the institution’s administrative affairs.  
  
## Research and documentation  
Library Alhazen conducts research and collects information through internal investigations, observations, field operations, and external contributions. Its scope is unrestricted, encompassing everything from scientific phenomena to the experiences and activities of individuals.  
  
Although the institution has documented most of the universe, certain subjects remain insufficiently understood. One such subject is [anechoi](../133.43/Anecho.md), individuals possessing abilities that Library Alhazen cannot explain within its current understanding of physics and thaumics. Their characteristics, abilities, and distribution are among the subjects of its ongoing investigations.  
  
Field operatives conduct research and observations outside the institution. When observing individuals, they are expected to remain discreet and avoid interfering with their activities, including refraining from intervening in dangerous circumstances.  
  
Information does not need to be independently verified before being documented. Library Alhazen distinguishes between different degrees of reliability and acknowledges that its records may contain incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, or conflicting information. Records are revised as additional information becomes available, with previous revisions retained.  
  
## Collections and accessibility  
Library Alhazen maintains an extensive collection of articles, documents, records, observations, and other written materials. Its collections are accessible to anyone capable of reaching the institution, and visitors are free to explore its facilities and consult its records.  
  
The institution also accepts contributions from visitors, including personal accounts and descriptions of their own thoughts and experiences. Such contributions provide information that cannot be obtained through external observation alone.  
  
Library Alhazen does not distribute its collections outside its infrastructure. Nevertheless, efforts are made to make its documentation understandable across [common languages](../410/Systematic%20linguistic%20resemblance.md).
