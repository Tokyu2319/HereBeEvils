A **wormhole** is a pair of points in space that are pulled together by [fine-controlled](../133.43/Manokinesis.md#Fine%20control) [manon](../133.43/Manon.md). Any two points within the influence radius of the controlling manon can be chosen as its endpoints.

## Formation  
A wormhole is formed when fine-controlled manon pulls two points of space together. Fine control has an influence radius of approximately 12 gigaparsecs, and the practitioner may choose any two points within it as the endpoints. The manon particles remain where they are while the pull is applied at the chosen points. A wormhole can therefore have endpoints that are beyond the practitioner's reach.  
  
A wormhole exists only while the manon is held. Wormholes are susceptible to the same threats as the fine-controlled manon that forms them: other manon fields, and gamma rays, which affect the manon particles themselves and not the surrounding influence radius.  
  
## Properties  
Everything can pass through a wormhole, including matter, light, and beings. Touching the edge of a wormhole destabilizes it and cuts anything that is crossing it at that moment.  
  
The size of a wormhole is determined by how much of the geometry of space is pulled together. Its duration depends on the practitioner or the armature holding it. A wormhole has no cost of its own, and requires only a practitioner with the talent or an armature.  
  
## Armature wormholes  
Fine control demands the utmost focus, so most wormholes are held by [armatures](../133.43/Armature.md). The armature carries a program that maintains the fine-controlled manon, and, as with other armature programs, damage may cause it to operate but not as intended.  
  
## Rings and shielding  
Because gamma rays can destabilize fine-controlled manon, the manon particles of an armature wormhole must be shielded. They are placed inside a ring, beneath the shielding. The shielding can be made of conventional tungsten and the framework of steel, with armatures used only for the programming parts.  
  
The ring also protects the rim of the wormhole from stray matter. Placing a wormhole within a protective ring is the more practical arrangement, though it is not required, and an armature can create endpoints outside the practitioner's reach. The rim of such a wormhole is unprotected.  
  
## Intersympantic travel  
Intersympantic travel can be achieved by stacking wormhole armatures along a path. A higher tier of manokinesis, **very fine control**, is used for this purpose. Very fine control is fine control with its influence radius expanded to approximately 10<sup>10<sup>72</sup></sup>/12 meters, a fraction of the distance given for reaching a neighboring [sympan](./Sympan.md).  
  
The coordinates of [sympanta](Library%20Alhazen꞉Sympanta%20coordinates.md) are a system for naming and are not used for travel.
