> **The accompanying image serves the specific purpose of nullifying Ramanujan’s ability.**  
  
Ramanujan’s ability can be triggered through any form of perception or exposure to information pertaining to him. This includes direct visual perception of Ramanujan himself, as well as the perception or dissemination of information through any medium, including audio recordings, visual records, video recordings, and written documents. The activation of the ability is not dependent on the perception or recognition of his name; rather, any form of exposure to information or representations pertaining to Ramanujan is independently sufficient to trigger it, regardless of whether his name is explicitly mentioned.  
  
Further access is restricted for all readers.
